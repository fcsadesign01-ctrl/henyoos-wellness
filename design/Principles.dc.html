Step 2 · The rule every direction follows

Hierarchy first: read top-down, like a pyramid

Each screen has one thing that matters most. It gets the size, the contrast and the only accent color. Everything else steps down.

LEVEL 1 · THE ONE THING
What this screen is for
Today's book · the amount due · the guest's name
LEVEL 2 · THE NEXT ACTION
One primary button
New appointment · Pay · Check in
LEVEL 3 · SUPPORTING DETAIL
Small, muted, scannable
Stats, times, line items, metadata
LEVEL 4 · ON DEMAND
Behind "More", hover or expand: logs, edits, settings
Six rules
1. One focal point per screen. It has the biggest type and the most contrast.
2. One primary button. The accent color goes there (and on the active state). Nowhere else.
3. Three text sizes, two greys. Title, body, caption. Secondary text is muted, never bold.
4. Color means status, never decoration. Booked, checked in, closed, attention.
5. Progressive disclosure. Rare actions ("I want to…", logs, refunds) live one click away.
6. Light and dark share the layout. Only the tokens swap; the hierarchy stays.
