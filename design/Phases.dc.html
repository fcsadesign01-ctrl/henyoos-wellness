Step 1 · Map · Phases

Product phases (from the guide) and the order we design screens

PHASE 0 · MVP
Foundation
Centers, rooms, tax, roles, employees, jobs and commissions, catalog, guests, schedules, pay periods.
PHASE 1 · MVP
Book & check in
Appointment book, new and existing guest booking, add-ons, request type, status colors, notes, repeat.
PHASE 2 · MVP
Checkout
POS, tenders, tips, receipts, retail, post-service upgrades, no-show fees, invoice log.
PHASE 3 · MVP
Memberships & gift cards
Month-to-month membership (two-invoice rule), packages, gift cards, loyalty points.
PHASE 4 · MVP
Controls
Reopen, edit with backdate prompt, refund, exchange, edit tips and commissions, 7-day lock.
PHASE 5
Payroll & time
Geofenced mobile clock, time edits, payroll report, commission calculation, provider app.
PHASE 6
Inventory
Purchase orders, receiving, audits, reconcile, consumable checkout, alerts, reports.
PHASE 7
Growth
Discounts, campaigns, segments, email, last-minute deals, two-way SMS, report center.
PHASE 8
Scale
Multi-center, cross-center redemption, enterprise reporting, online booking.
Proposed screen design order
1. App shell + navigation
2. Appointment book (day, by provider)
3. New appointment panel
4. Guest profile
5. Take Payment
6. Invoice + "I want to…" (reopen, refund)
7. Manager morning review
8. Membership sale
Then: team, payroll, inventory, marketing, reports, setup

• Status colors driven by one legend
• Desktop + tablet layouts; provider app on mobile
• Keyboard and barcode-scanner checkout
• Language and currency as settings (Arabic / RTL possible)
