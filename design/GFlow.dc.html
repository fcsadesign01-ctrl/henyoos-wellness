What staff see
• Lands straight on the appointment book as Booked (white), tagged "Online".
• The guest is matched by phone, or a new profile is made with the referral source.
• A reply of C to the 48 h text turns the block Confirmed (green).
• The online booking queue is only for requests that need a staff decision, e.g. groups.
Rules it follows (from the guide)
• Price by guest type: intro, member, non-member.
• Only providers who can perform the service and are scheduled.
• Add-ons never add time.
• No-show / late cancel: 50% fee to the held card.
• SMS consent is captured before any text is sent.
Per-spa settings
• Logo and accent color (henyoOS purple by default; shown here).
• Country, which sets currency and phone code: UAE · AED · +971 shown.
• Cancellation window and fee %.
• Which services can be booked online.
• Small "Powered by henyoOS" footer.
